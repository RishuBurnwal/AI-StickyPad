Saving & history

SavingAutomatic. Text, formatting, pictures, size, position, colour and timers all come back next launch
Save a copy💾 on a note, then pick the format. It lands in Notes\saved\<format>\ next to the app, named after the note's title — never inside AppData
Which formatsMarkdown (headings, lists, bold and highlights carried over as ==highlight==), PDF, JSON, a standalone HTML page, or plain text
Same name twiceNothing is overwritten — the second file becomes Name (2).md, then Name (3).md
Deleted a note?🗑 on the bar or Ctrl Alt D — the last 200 deleted notes, with Restore. A readable copy of each is also kept in Notes\history\

AI actions

✦ on the barOpens the AI window: Prompts (create / edit / delete reusable skills by title + prompt) and Settings
✦ on a noteLists your saved prompts plus Custom prompt… at the end. Picking one sends the prompt + the note's content to the AI and rewrites the note
PicturesImages are swapped for [IMG:n] tokens before sending and put back where they fit — nothing the AI does can lose a picture
Undo / redo↶ ↷ on the note step through every AI change, as many times as you like (100 steps kept)
SettingsPick the provider + model, Fetch models from API, Test model (one tiny request — live / dead, with the real error: bad key, rate limit, payment required), add a custom model (any OpenAI-compatible URL)
KeysMultiple keys per provider — when one hits a rate limit the next is tried automatically. The count shows next to each provider. Keys live in .env next to the app and can be added / removed from the UI too

Offline dictation 🎤

What it isSpeech-to-text that runs entirely on your machine — no internet, no GPU, no cloud. Hindi, Hinglish (mixed Hindi + English) and English
One-time setuppython stt\setup.py (or option 4 in main.py). Downloads the speech model once (default small — the first size whose Devanagari Hindi is actually good); after that everything is offline
Using it🎤 on a note → tap once to start, tap again to stop. Mode (mic/speaker/both) and language come from the bar's 🎙 panel — defaults: both sides + auto-detect. Text appears live in the strip under the note; finished sentences are inserted into the note
InterruptionDictation is listening while an AI answer is being generated — if you start speaking, the AI request is cancelled instantly (barge-in). Your voice always wins
Echo guardIf the speakers are playing something, dictation suppresses that sound automatically — the speakers can never end up in your notes. Speaker silent → no extra cost
Language modesHindi/Hinglish writes Hindi words in Devanagari and English words in Latin (e.g. "मैं market जा रहा हूँ"); English; Auto-detect decides per utterance
Too slow / too heavy?Set STT_MODEL=base (faster, weaker Hindi) or STT_MODEL=tiny before launching the app
PrivacyThe microphone audio never leaves the process — transcription happens in a local Python engine beside the app

Call mode 📞 (PU / SU speakers)

What it doesTranscribes a live call (Zoom, Google Meet, WhatsApp) into a note: PU = you (the mic), SU = the far side (captured digitally from the speakers — perfect quality, no echo problem: the two never share a channel)
Starting itOpen the bar's 🎙 → AI window → Dictation tab → Listener = Both sides (or Only speaker). Then 🎤 on a note starts it: PU = you (mic), SU = the call (speakers). Each line lands in the note with a coloured speaker label
RenamingOpen AI → Speakers: rename PU or SU once and every label in every note updates at the same time — no per-line editing. Colours are per-speaker and customizable there too
One definable userIn a meeting you can single out one extra speaker: give them a name + a cue phrase (something they say, like their name). Any call line containing that phrase is tagged with their name instead of SU
PrivacyBoth channels are transcribed locally. Nothing is uploaded

Timers

WhereOne on the control bar, plus an independent one on every note (⏱)
Choose a timeClick the digits — a menu offers 1 min up to 2 hours. Picking one sets and starts it
Any other timeCustom… at the bottom of that menu lets you type it: 7 = 7 minutes, 1:30 = 1 min 30 sec
5 15 25Presets — one click sets and starts. Widen a narrow note to see them
▶ ❚❚Start / pause
↺Reset to the last duration
When it endsBeeps, a Windows notification, and the clock pulses red. Hidden notes pop back into view
AccuracyTimers run off a wall-clock deadline, so they survive being hidden, throttled, or a restart

Click-through mode

What it doesNotes stay fully visible on top, but the mouse passes straight through them into the app underneath
Not the same as hidingNothing disappears — this is the "read my notes while I work" mode
Getting backThe control bar stays clickable, and Ctrl Alt T works from anywhere
Everything is saved automatically — text, formatting, pictures, position, size, colour, pin state and timers all come back exactly as you left them.
Hid the bar and forgot? Ctrl Alt B brings it back.
